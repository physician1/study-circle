Our purpose

Progress, at your pace.

We make independent study feel a little less independent.

What we do

Study Circle brings students together for focused work and practical conversations about learning. Members choose their own goals and support one another through regular check-ins.

Our sessions combine individual study time with optional discussion. You can work on a reading assignment, practice a skill, or plan your next project.

Who can join?

Any student looking for a consistent study routine is welcome. No membership fee or previous experience is required.

Our shared agreements

  • Respect quiet work periods.
  • Ask before offering advice.
  • Make space for different learning styles.
  • Explain concepts while letting others complete their own work.
  • Keep personal information private.
